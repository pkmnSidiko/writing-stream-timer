Roadmap

The toolbox is growing. Here's a look at some of the things we're considering, building, and brewing next.

What's brewing

A few things on the way

Project Tracker

Keep an eye on project goals, progress, deadlines, and external writing links without giving the tool access to the manuscript.

Prompt Generator

A flexible Who / What / When / Where / Why / How system for generating prompts, ingredients, and writing challenges.

Writing Sprints

Focused sprint tools for solo writing, body doubling, and group sessions, with optional word-count progress.

Challenge Tools

Build and track writing challenges with dates, cadences, themes, ingredients, constraints, goals, and submission links.

Stream Widgets

Small browser-source widgets for current activity, writing goals, session progress, breaks, and other stream-friendly displays.

Story Utilities

Randomizers, dice and roll tools, countdowns, sensory prompts, writing math, session notes, and other little helpers.

The philosophy

Built to stay useful

✦

Writer-first, platform-neutral, local-first

The goal is a collection of small tools that can work in a browser, on a website, in OBS or Streamlabs, or alongside whatever writing software a writer already uses. They should not need to read the manuscript to be useful.
